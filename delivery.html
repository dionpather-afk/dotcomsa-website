Delivery & returns

We deliver anywhere in South Africa.

Where we deliver

We deliver to every province in South Africa, by courier to your door.

Delivery cost

Delivery is R100.00 per order.

How long it takes

Orders usually arrive in 3 to 5 working days after payment is confirmed. Outlying areas may take a little longer.

Returns and exchanges

  • Wrong size? Contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll help you exchange it.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags attached.
  • If an item arrives damaged or faulty, let us know straight away and we'll make it right, in line with the Consumer Protection Act.

To start a return, contact us with your order number.

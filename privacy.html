Privacy policy

How DotComSA looks after your personal information.

Draft for client review.

What we collect

When you order, you give us your name, cellphone number and delivery address. You send these to us yourself in a WhatsApp message or email.

What we don't collect

This website does not store your details on a server. Your cart is saved only in your own browser so it remembers your items.

How we use it

We use your details only to confirm your order, arrange payment and deliver it. We share your name, number and address with our courier for delivery and nothing else.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us on 082 049 3930 or xolanimayor@gmail.com.
